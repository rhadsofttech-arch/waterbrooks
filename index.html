About Waterbrook

A Foundation Built on Excellence

For over two decades, Waterbrook Schools has combined academic rigour with genuine warmth — shaping confident, well-rounded children ready for the world ahead.

About Waterbrook Schools

Waterbrook is a premier British-curriculum school in Lagos, dedicated to nurturing curious, capable, and compassionate learners from Early Years through Primary. Our classrooms blend structured academic excellence with the freedom to explore, question, and grow.

Est. Reputation for Excellence British Curriculum

Our Mission

To cultivate a nurturing academic environment where every child discovers their potential and builds the character to pursue it.

Our Vision

To be Lagos’s most trusted name in early and primary education — recognised for excellence, safety, and heart.

Our Learning Philosophy

We believe children learn best when they feel safe, seen, and challenged in equal measure. Every lesson at Waterbrook is designed around inquiry, play, and purposeful discovery — guided by teachers who know each child by name and by nature.
